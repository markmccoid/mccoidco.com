Getting Started

LAABS Audio is an audiobook player for iPhone. It was built with Audiobookshelf in mind, but you don't need an Audiobookshelf server to use it. You can import audiobook files straight onto your phone and listen to them, connect to your own Audiobookshelf server, or do both.

Start by downloading LAABS Audio from the App Store. Then pick a starting point. You can add the other one whenever you like.

No server needed

Import your own audiobooks

Skip the server sign-in and import audiobooks from the Files app or iCloud Drive. LAABS Audio plays m4b, m4a, mp3, aac, wav, and flac files.

Imported books get chapters, cover art, and CarPlay, and they show up on their own Imported bookshelf. Bookmarks, clips, and ambient audio all work with imported books.

Audiobookshelf v2.26.0+

Connect to Audiobookshelf

Sign in to your own Audiobookshelf server to stream, download, and sync your listening progress back to the server. Playlists you make in LAABS Audio sync to Audiobookshelf too.

Your server must be running version 2.26.0 or higher.

🤝 You can do both

Importing books and using Audiobookshelf aren't an either/or choice. Sign in to your server and you can still import audiobooks from Files or iCloud Drive. Start with imported books and you can connect a server later without losing them. Server books and imported books sit side by side in the same app, with imported ones collected on the Imported shelf.

Connecting to Audiobookshelf

⚠️ Requires Audiobookshelf v2.26.0 or higher

Audiobookshelf changed its authentication system in v2.26.0, and LAABS Audio only supports the new system. If your server runs an older version, sign-in will fail. Update your server before you connect. Your Audiobookshelf version is shown in the server's web interface under Settings.

1

Enter your server address

Use the same address you use to reach Audiobookshelf in a browser, including the port if there is one. For example, https://books.yourdomain.com or http://192.168.1.10:13378.

2

Sign in

Use your Audiobookshelf username and password. Your credentials are stored securely on your iPhone and are only ever sent to your server.

3

Pick a library

If your account can see more than one Audiobookshelf library, choose the one you want. You can switch libraries later, and your shelves and playlists follow the library you're in.

Streaming and downloading

Books from your server can be streamed or downloaded. Open a book and tap the cloud button beside the cover to download its audio and cover art to your iPhone.

The play button always tells you which mode you're in: Stream or local playback. Downloaded books keep playing with no connection, and any progress you make offline syncs back to Audiobookshelf when you reconnect.

Downloading matters for a few features. Exporting clip audio and transcriptions both need the book on your phone (see Bookmarks & Clips).

Bookshelves

The Home screen is a stack of bookshelves. Each shelf is a row of covers you can swipe through, with listening progress under each book. Tap the progress badge to flip between time listened and time left.

Not every shelf works the same way. LAABS Audio has three kinds, and knowing which is which tells you where its books come from and whether it syncs with Audiobookshelf.

Types of bookshelves

Derived

Automatic shelves

Built for you from your library and listening. You don't add books to these; they fill themselves.

  • Continue Listening: books in progress
  • Recently Added: newest books in your library
  • Discover: a rotating pick of books you haven't started
  • Downloaded: books stored on your iPhone
  • Imported: books you imported from Files or iCloud Drive
Playlist

Audiobookshelf playlists

Shelves that are backed by a playlist on your Audiobookshelf server, and stay in sync with it.

  • Playlists you already have in Audiobookshelf appear as shelves.
  • A new playlist shelf made in LAABS Audio is created on your server as a playlist.
  • Adding or removing books on either side shows up on the other.
Device only

Local shelves

Shelves stored only on your iPhone. They never touch your server, so they're good for personal groupings like a bedtime shelf or a research pile, and they work without Audiobookshelf at all.

How playlists sync with Audiobookshelf

A playlist shelf is a live view of an Audiobookshelf playlist. Changes you make in LAABS Audio are written to the playlist on your server, and changes made on the server (in the web app or another client) appear on the shelf the next time LAABS Audio refreshes your library.

  • Audiobookshelf playlists belong to your user and to a single library, so you'll see the playlists for the library you're currently in.
  • Hiding a playlist shelf only hides it in LAABS Audio. The playlist stays on your server untouched.
  • If you aren't connected to a server, use device-only shelves instead.

Adding books to a shelf

On a book's detail page, tap the bookshelf button at the top of the column beside the cover. Choose the playlist or device-only shelves you want the book on. A book can sit on as many shelves as you like.

Rearranging, hiding, and showing shelves

Go to Settings › Bookshelves to manage every shelf in one list.

  • Reorder: drag a shelf by its handle (≡) to move it up or down. Home updates to match.
  • Hide or show: each shelf is marked Shown or Hidden. Tap a shelf to change it. Hidden shelves aren't deleted, and you can bring them back anytime.
  • Visible Only: filter the list down to the shelves currently on Home.
  • New Shelf: create a playlist shelf or a device-only shelf.

The badge under each name tells you its type: Derived, Playlist, or device only.

Bookmarks & Clips

A bookmark marks a single moment in a book. A clip is simply a bookmark extended: it has a start and an end, so it captures a whole passage instead of a single point. Clips live in the same bookmark list, can carry the same notes, and can be up to one hour long.

Bookmark

One position. Jump back to it anytime.

Clip

A start and an end. Replay it, export the audio, or turn it into text.

Creating a bookmark

While listening, tap the bookmark button in the row of controls at the bottom of the player. The bookmark is saved at your current position. Give it a title and an optional note now, or edit it later.

Turning a bookmark into a clip

When you add a bookmark, choose to make it a clip. The clip editor opens with your position as the starting point.

  • Selected range at the top shows the start, duration, and end.
  • Starting position nudges the start back or forward by 1 minute, 10 seconds, or 1 second.
  • Duration sets the length with a slider or the same nudge buttons. Lock it to move the start without changing the length.
  • Preview plays the range. Last 5s replays just the ending so you can trim precisely.

Making a clip from Read-Along text

If the book has a transcript, you can clip straight from the words. In Read-Along, long-press a sentence, tap more sentences to extend the selection, then save. The clip's start and end are taken from the text, and its words come along with it.

Exporting clip audio and transcriptions

📥 The book must be downloaded

Exporting a clip's audio and transcribing a clip both work from the audio file on your iPhone. If you're streaming the book, download it first (the cloud button on the book's detail page), then come back to the clip. Imported books are already on your phone.

Open a saved clip from the book's bookmark list to see its export options:

  • Export audio: saves the clip's range as an M4A file you can share, send, or save to Files.
  • Transcribe and export text: turns the clip's audio into text. If the book already has a transcript, the clip's text is ready instantly; otherwise LAABS Audio transcribes it on your iPhone. The export includes the book title, bookmark title, clip range, and the text.
  • Notes: stay with the bookmark on your device, whether or not you export.

🔒 Private by design

Transcription runs entirely on your iPhone. No audio or text is uploaded anywhere. On-device transcription requires iOS 26.

Ambient Audio

Ambient audio plays a second, quieter track underneath the narration: soft music, rain, a fireplace, focus sounds, whatever helps you listen. The track loops for as long as the book plays, and it has its own volume so the narrator always stays on top.

How it works

1

Import your tracks

Go to Settings › Ambient Audio and add audio files from your iPhone. Import as many as you like so you can switch between different sound beds.

2

Pick a track in the player

Open the Ambient Audio sheet from the player and choose a track. It starts looping right away behind the book.

3

Set the mix

Drag Ambient Volume until it sits comfortably under the narrator. Then close the sheet and listen.

Controls

  • Ambient Volume is separate from the narration, so you can turn the background down without touching the book.
  • Fine Volume narrows the slider's range so you can make small adjustments at very low levels, where most ambient tracks sound best.
  • Ambient Position moves where the loop is playing. Drag it to skip a part of the track you don't like.
  • The ambient bar in the player shows what's playing. Use its button to pause just the ambient track, or tap × to remove it for a narration-only session.

How it behaves with your book

  • Pause the book and the ambient track pauses with it. Resume, and both pick up together.
  • The track is attached to the book you're listening to, so it comes back when you return to that book.
  • Removing a track from a book doesn't delete it. It stays in Settings › Ambient Audio for next time.
  • Ambient audio works with streamed, downloaded, and imported books alike.

💡 Ideas

Rain or ocean sounds for bedtime listening. Lo-fi or instrumental music behind non-fiction. A crackling fire for a fantasy series. Brown noise to keep you focused on a commute.
