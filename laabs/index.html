New · Read-Along

Hear it. See it. Never lose your place.

Open Read-Along from the player and the words follow the narrator, highlighted as they're spoken. Scroll ahead, look back, tap a sentence to jump there. Your audio never skips a beat.

Transcript

The words the narrator actually said

Transcribe a downloaded book right on your iPhone, or let LAABS pick up a transcript stored with the book on your server.

EPUB

The publisher's own pages

Read the real book, with the narrated sentence highlighted. Choose your font, theme, margins, and line spacing.

PDF

Pages that turn with the audio

The PDF follows the narration, with a highlight box on the current paragraph. Tap Listen from page N to start anywhere.

  • Tap to jump. Tap any sentence to move the audio there. A Go back button undoes a wrong tap.
  • Resume following. Scroll away to browse; one tap brings you back to the narrator.
  • Speed in reach. Quarter-step speeds from 0.75× to 2.5× without leaving the reader.
  • Bookmarks in the margin. Your saved bookmarks and clips show up beside the text.
  • Private by design. On-device transcription uploads nothing. Export a finished transcript as an EPUB.
  • Read before it's done. Transcription runs at about 25× real time and resumes where it stopped.

On-device transcription requires iOS 26 and a downloaded book. Server transcripts work on any supported iPhone. EPUB and PDF Read-Along need alignment files in the book's Audiobookshelf folder, which LAABS Audio Align creates for you.

For self-hosters who want more

Everything lives with the book.

Two optional companions extend LAABS Audio into your server. They write small files into each book's own Audiobookshelf folder, so transcripts, alignments, and bookmarks survive database rebuilds, renames, and moves.

Mac & Windows

LAABS Audio Align

A desktop app that does the heavy lifting for Read-Along. Sign in to your server, pick a book, and Align transcribes the audio and lines it up with the book's EPUB, then publishes the results straight into the book's folder.

  • Book Transcripts for every iPhone, not just iOS 26
  • EPUB alignment so the real book highlights in time
  • Queue whole books; it never transcribes a book twice
  • Finds your library on a mounted share, so nothing is downloaded

Docker sidecar

LAABS Companion

A tiny self-hosted service that runs next to Audiobookshelf and keeps every bookmark and clip in sync across your devices, including titles, notes, and clip ranges.

  • Bookmarks stored with the book, invisible to Audiobookshelf's scanner
  • No database, no extra accounts; uses your Audiobookshelf sign-in
  • Each user can only reach their own bookmarks
  • Syncs automatically, offline edits included; never wipes device bookmarks

The app in action

See it for yourself.

Every screen is designed around real listening habits.

Home Screen

Your library, organized your way.

Shelves for Continue Listening, Next Up, Recently Added, and anything you create. Progress, time remaining, and offline status are all visible at a glance.

Now Playing

One player, everything in reach.

Chapters, skip controls, sleep timer, bookmarks, Read-Along, ambient audio, and speed, all one tap away. The ambient track sits right on top so you always know what's playing behind the narration.

Book Detail

Stream or download, and see what's ready to read.

Stream straight from your server or download for offline listening. The app always shows which mode you're in, and a Transcribed badge tells you when Read-Along is ready.

Clips

Clip up to an hour of audio from any book.

Nudge the start by a second or a minute, set the length, preview it, and save. Clips become bookmarks you can export as audio, turn into text, or revisit any time.
